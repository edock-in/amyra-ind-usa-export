Part 1

Decide what you are selling, and who makes it

Nothing official yet. This part is about picking a fight you can win and finding the hands that will make the thing.

1
Pick the product
Choose something people abroad already buy, that India makes well, and that survives a long boat ride.
India · your desk

In plain words

You are looking for a product that is already selling in your target country, where the current sellers are beatable, and where India has a genuine cost or craft advantage. Think brass and copper kitchenware, cotton bedding, spices, jute bags, handloom textiles, wooden toys, yoga props, incense, herbal teas.

Good first products are small, light, hard to break, and not too cheap. Shipping and Amazon's fees eat a fixed slice of every sale, so a product that sells for under about fifteen dollars rarely leaves any profit for you.

Think of it like opening a food stall at a fair in another city. You do not invent a new dish. You pick a dish people there already queue for, and you make it better or cheaper than the stall next door.

Why it matters

  • Every later step costs money and months. A wrong product wastes all of it.
  • Some categories carry heavy rules abroad (food, anything you put on your skin, children's items, electronics). Step 7 gets much harder if you pick one of these first.
  • Amazon charges a percentage of the sale price plus a storage and delivery fee per unit. The math has to work before you order anything.

Jargon decoded

Niche
A narrow corner of a big market, such as "copper water bottles" instead of "kitchenware".
Private label
Selling a product a factory already makes, under your own brand name. Most first-time Amazon sellers do this.
BSR
Best Sellers Rank. Amazon's popularity score on every listing. A lower number means it sells more. Free way to guess demand.Tools like Helium 10 or Jungle Scout turn this into estimated monthly sales.
Unit economics
The profit on one single unit after every fee, tax, duty and shipping cost is subtracted. If one unit does not make money, a thousand will not either.
Watch out: avoid products that are fragile, oversized, battery-powered, or that need to be eaten or applied to skin for your first attempt. Each of these adds a layer of rules or breakage you do not need while learning.
2
Find the maker in India
Locate a factory or workshop that can make your product consistently, and get samples in your hands.
India · factories and clusters

In plain words

India makes most things in clusters: brass in Moradabad, textiles in Tiruppur and Panipat, leather in Kanpur, wooden toys in Channapatna, spices in Kerala and Guntur. Start with the cluster for your product. Directories like IndiaMART and TradeIndia, export promotion councils, and simply visiting the town all work.

Ask for samples from three or more makers. Compare quality, price per unit, minimum order size, and how quickly they answer messages. The one who replies clearly at 9 pm on a Sunday is often the one to work with.

Think of it like hiring a tailor for a wedding. You would never place the full order without seeing a stitched sample first, and you would want to know they can deliver forty outfits, not just one.

Why it matters

  • Your brand's reputation abroad is only as good as this factory's worst batch.
  • Step 6 needs the factory to print your brand permanently on the product or its packaging. Confirm now that they can.
  • Step 7 may need the factory itself to be registered with a foreign authority (food is the classic case). A cooperative factory saves you weeks.

Jargon decoded

MOQ
Minimum Order Quantity. The smallest batch a factory will make for you. Negotiable, especially if you pay a little more per unit.
OEM / white label
The factory makes its existing product and puts your brand on it (white label). OEM means they make it to your custom design.
Golden sample
One approved sample you both sign off on. Every unit in the real order must match it. Keep one at home.
EPC
Export Promotion Council. Government-backed bodies for each industry (handicrafts, spices, apparel, and so on) that list genuine exporters and manufacturers. Membership also unlocks some export benefits later.
Watch out: agree in writing who owns the mould, the design and the packaging artwork. Otherwise the factory can legally sell "your" product to the next buyer who walks in.
Part 2

Get your papers in order

Three sets of paperwork: one to own your name abroad, one so India lets your goods leave, and one so Amazon and the foreign tax office know who you are.

3
Claim your brand name in the target country
Register your brand name where the customers are, not just in India. Amazon will ask for proof of this.
Target country · trademark office

In plain words

A trademark is the government writing down that a name (and often a logo) belongs to you for a certain kind of product. Trademarks are country by country. An Indian trademark means nothing in an American court, so you file one in the country you will sell in. For the US that is the federal trademark officeUSPTO.

The full registration takes a year or more, but you do not need to wait. Amazon accepts a filed application that is still being examined. So file early, get your application number, and move on.

Think of it like booking your shop's name on the high street of a foreign city. Until you book it, anyone can put up a sign with your name and sell copies of your product under it.

Why it matters

  • Without it you cannot join Amazon's brand-owner programmeBrand Registry, which unlocks rich product pages, protection against copycats, and better advertising tools.
  • Copycats on Amazon are common. Owning the name is how you get their listings removed.
  • Foreign applicants at the US office must file through a US-licensed attorney. Budget for that fee on top of the government fee.

Jargon decoded

Trademark
Legal ownership of a brand name or logo for specific kinds of goods, in one country.
Class
The category bucket your goods fall into. Copper bottles and cotton bedsheets sit in different classes, and each class is a separate fee.There are 45 classes worldwide, called Nice classes.
Pending vs registered
Pending means filed and waiting. Registered means approved. Amazon accepts pending for Brand Registry in most countries, the US included.
Serial number
The number the US office gives your application on day one. This is what you type into Amazon.
Amazon IP Accelerator
Amazon's list of vetted trademark lawyers. Filing through one gets you into Brand Registry faster, sometimes within days.
Watch out: search the target country's trademark database before you print a single label. If someone already owns a similar name in your class, pick another name now. Rebranding after production is painful.
4
Get India's permission to export
Three things: an exporter's ID, a tax registration with a promise letter so you skip upfront tax, and a code from your bank.
India · government portals and your bank

In plain words

First, an exporter's ID. India gives every exporter a ten-digit numberIEC. It is issued online by the foreign trade departmentDGFT, costs a small fee, and is yours for life as long as you confirm your details once a year. Without it, no shipment leaves the country.

Second, a tax registration plus a promise letter. You register your business for goods and services taxGST. Exports are meant to be tax-free, but the default system makes you pay the tax firstIGST and claim it back months later. To skip that, you sign a promise letterLUT on the tax portal saying "I am exporting and I will bring the money home". Then you ship without paying the tax at all. The letter is renewed every April.

Third, a bank code. Customs needs to know which bank will receive your foreign money. Your bank gives you a codeAD Code and you register it on the customs portalICEGATE. Without this, the port cannot generate your export document.

Think of it like getting a passport (the exporter's ID), a signed declaration at the airport that you will come back so you skip a deposit (the promise letter), and telling the airline which account to refund to (the bank code).

Why it matters

  • Every shipping document at the port needs the exporter's ID and the bank code. Get them before the goods are ready, not on packing day.
  • The promise letter keeps your cash. Paying 18 percent tax upfront on a large first order, then waiting for a refund, can sink a small business.
  • The promise you make comes due in Step 11. The bank's proof that the money arrived is how you keep it.

Jargon decoded

IEC
Importer Exporter Code. Your 10-digit exporter's ID from the government. One-time, online, linked to your PAN.Must be re-confirmed every year between April and June or it gets deactivated.
DGFT
Directorate General of Foreign Trade. The department that issues the IEC and later records proof that your export money came home.
GST
Goods and Services Tax. India's sales tax. You need a GST number to export as a business and to sign the promise letter.
IGST
The version of GST charged when goods cross a state or national border. Exporters either pay it and reclaim it, or skip it with an LUT.
LUT
Letter of Undertaking. A one-page promise filed online each financial year. It lets you export without paying IGST upfront.Filed as form GST RFD-11 on the GST portal. Free.
AD Code
Authorised Dealer Code. A 14-digit code from the bank branch that holds your business current account. Register it once per port on ICEGATE.
ICEGATE
Indian customs' online portal. This is where the AD code is registered and where your shipping bill is filed.
Shipping bill
The customs form generated for every export shipment. It proves the goods left India. You will need it for tax refunds and to prove you kept your LUT promise.
Watch out: open a separate business current account before applying for the bank code. Mixing personal and export money makes the bank's remittance proofs (Step 11) messy, and makes tax filing harder.
5
Open your Amazon shop, and tell the US tax office who you are
A seller account for that country's Amazon, plus a US tax ID and a form that says "I am not American, do not tax me twice".
Online · Amazon and the US tax office

In plain words

Each country's Amazon is a separate shop with its own seller account. Indian residents sign up through Amazon's export programmeAmazon Global Selling, which lets you register for the US, UK, Europe and more with Indian documents: your PAN, a passport or Aadhaar, a business bank account, and your exporter's ID from Step 4. Amazon will do a video call to check you are a real person.

For the US, two extra things. A US tax number for your businessEIN, which the US tax officeIRS gives for free to foreign businesses over fax or phone. And a short form that declares you live outside the USW-8BEN / W-8BEN-E, so Amazon does not hold back US tax from your payouts. You fill it inside Amazon's seller dashboard; it is called the tax interview.

Think of it like renting a stall in an American mall. The mall (Amazon) wants your ID and bank details. The city (the IRS) wants to know who is trading, gives you a trader number, and lets you sign a note saying your taxes are paid back home so they do not collect them here too.

Why it matters

  • Skip the foreign-status form and Amazon may withhold up to 30 percent of every payout for US tax.
  • The US tax number is also what customs brokers and freight forwarders ask for when they set you up as the importer in Step 9.
  • Amazon's identity check is strict. Name, address and bank account must match across every document, letter for letter.

Jargon decoded

Marketplace
One country's Amazon site, such as Amazon.com (US) or Amazon.co.uk. Each is a separate account, catalogue and fee schedule.
Seller Central
The dashboard where you manage listings, stock, ads and payouts. Your office, basically.
Professional plan
The paid monthly seller plan (about 40 dollars a month in the US). Needed for ads, brand tools and selling in most categories.
EIN
Employer Identification Number. A US tax ID for a business. Free from the IRS. Foreign businesses apply with form SS-4 by fax or phone; there is no online route without a US social security number.
W-8BEN
The form an individual signs to say "I live outside the US". W-8BEN-E is the same form for a company or LLP. It stops US tax being deducted from your Amazon payouts.Valid for three years, then you re-sign.
Tax interview
Amazon's built-in questionnaire that produces your W-8BEN for you. No paper form to mail.
Watch out: apply for the EIN early. The fax route can take weeks, and a bad first application (wrong entity type, missing signature) starts the clock again. You do not need a US company to get one.
Part 3

Make it, then prove it

Now the factory runs. Then you prove two things: to Amazon that the brand is really yours, and to the target country that the product is safe to sell there.

The order that trips people up

Amazon's brand programme wants real photographs of your product and packaging with your brand name printed on them permanently. Not a design file, not a sticker, not a mock-up. So the first production run has to happen before you can finish registering the brand, and the trademark (Step 3) has to be filed before that. File first, print second, photograph third.

6
Run the first real batch, brand on it, and photograph it
Make the product with your name printed on it, then use those photos to prove to Amazon that the brand is yours.
India · the factory, then online

In plain words

Place your first order with the factory from Step 2. Your brand name must be part of the product itself: engraved, embroidered, moulded, printed on the box, woven into a label. Something that cannot peel off.

When the first units are ready, photograph them plainly on a table: the product showing the brand, the packaging showing the brand, and a shot of the two together. Upload these to Amazon's brand-owner programmeBrand Registry along with your trademark application number from Step 3. Approval usually takes a few days to a couple of weeks.

Think of it like proving a jersey is your team's. Anyone can hold up a drawing of a logo. Only the real jersey, with the crest stitched in, settles it.

Why it matters

  • Brand Registry is what lets you build the picture-rich product page in Step 8, run the better ads in Step 10, and pull down copycat listings.
  • It also gives you a brand-owner shortcut on barcodes and lets you lock your product pages so others cannot edit your photos and text.
  • A small first run (a few hundred units) is fine here. Its job is to exist, be photographed and be tested by real customers.

Jargon decoded

Brand Registry
Amazon's programme for brand owners. Free to join. Needs a trademark (filed or registered) and real photos of the branded product.
Permanently affixed
Amazon's phrase for branding that cannot be removed: printed, engraved, woven, moulded, or on a sewn-in label. A paper sticker does not count.
GTIN / UPC
The barcode number on your packaging. Amazon needs one per product to create a listing. Buy genuine ones from GS1 (the barcode body), or ask Amazon for an exemption as a registered brand owner.Barcodes bought from resellers are often rejected.
Pre-shipment inspection
Paying an independent inspector to check the finished batch at the factory before it ships. A few thousand rupees that can save a container of returns.
Watch out: the brand name on the product, on the trademark filing and on your Amazon account must be spelled identically. "Karigar Home" and "KarigarHome" can get your application rejected.
7
Pass the target country's product rules
Some products need that country's safety tests, labels or registrations before Amazon will list them. Food, herbal products and toys are the strict ones.
Target country · regulators and test labs

In plain words

Every country has its own idea of what makes a product safe and honestly labelled. India's rules do not travel with the goods. For a plain cotton cushion cover the US asks for little beyond a label naming the fibre, the country of origin and how to wash it. For anything you eat, rub on your skin, or give to a child, there is a proper checklist.

Food and spices going to the US: the factory must be registered with the US food authorityFDA, you need a US-based contact person for that registration, every shipment is announced in advance, and the label must follow the American format (nutrition panel, allergens, ingredient order).

Ayurvedic and herbal products: in the US these are sold as dietary supplements, not medicines. You cannot say a product treats or cures anything. Labels follow supplement rules, and heavy-metal testing is wise because Ayurvedic imports get checked for it.

Toys and children's items: tested by an approved lab against the US toy standard, a written safety certificateCPC, and a permanent tracking label on the product. Amazon will not open the toy category until it sees these documents.

Think of it like a driving licence. Your Indian licence lets you drive in India. To drive in America for the long term you take their test, follow their signs and carry their paperwork, even though you already know how to drive.

Why it matters

  • Amazon "gates" regulated categories. You upload the certificates first, then you are allowed to list.
  • Customs can seize or destroy a shipment that fails labelling rules. That is your whole batch and your shipping money gone.
  • Every import into the US must be marked with its country of origin. "Made in India" on the product or its packaging is not optional.

Jargon decoded

FDA
Food and Drug Administration. The US authority for food, supplements, cosmetics and medicines.
Food facility registration
The factory registers with the FDA before shipping food to the US. Foreign factories must name a US Agent, a US-based contact the FDA can call. Renewed every two years.
Prior Notice
A heads-up filed with the FDA before each food shipment arrives. Your forwarder or broker usually files it.
Dietary supplement
The US legal bucket for herbal and Ayurvedic products. Allowed to describe general wellness, never to claim to treat a disease.
MoCRA
The 2022 US cosmetics law. Soaps, oils and creams sold as cosmetics need the facility registered and each product listed with the FDA.
CPSC / CPC
Consumer Product Safety Commission, the US toy and children's product regulator. A CPC (Children's Product Certificate) is the one-page certificate you write, backed by a lab test against standard ASTM F963.
Gated category
An Amazon category locked until you upload approvals. Toys, supplements, food and topicals are commonly gated.
Country of origin marking
The permanent "Made in India" on the product or its packaging, required by US customs on every import.
Watch out: ask a compliance consultant or your freight forwarder what your exact product needs before the factory prints packaging. Reprinting labels for a finished batch is a common and expensive mistake.
Part 4

Put it on the shelf and start selling

Build the page customers will see, get the boxes into Amazon's warehouse, then launch small and let the market tell you what it thinks.

8
Build the listing
Photos that sell, a picture-rich lower section, and the exact words that shoppers in that country type into the search box.
Online · Seller Central

In plain words

Your listing is your shop window, your salesperson and your packaging all in one. Shoppers decide in seconds from the main photo. Amazon wants that first photo on a pure white background with the product filling most of the frame. The remaining photos show scale, use, details and what is in the box. One short video helps a lot.

Lower on the page, brand owners get a rich section of banners, comparison charts and story imagesA+ Content. This is where you tell the story of the artisan, the material, the village.

Then the words. Americans search for a "comforter", not a "razai"; a "throw pillow", not a "cushion". Use a keyword tool or Amazon's own search data to find the exact phrases, and put them in your title, bullet points and hidden search terms.

Think of it like setting up a stall where the customer can only see your photos and read your sign, cannot touch anything, and walks past two hundred rival stalls in a minute. Your photos have to do the touching for them.

Why it matters

  • Amazon's search engine ranks you by how often shoppers click and buy. A weak listing never gets shown, so ads in Step 10 are wasted on it.
  • Words matter for compliance too. Claims like "cures", "antibacterial" or "organic" need proof or approval in the target country.
  • Photos taken in India can look foreign to a US shopper. Show the product in a home that looks like theirs.

Jargon decoded

ASIN
Amazon Standard Identification Number. The unique code Amazon gives every product page. Yours is created when you list.
Main image
The first photo. Pure white background, product filling about 85 percent of the frame, at least 1000 pixels so shoppers can zoom.
A+ Content
The picture-rich section below the bullet points. Only brand owners (Step 6) can add it. Free.
Backend keywords
Hidden search terms you type into the listing that shoppers never see but the search engine reads.
Brand Analytics
Amazon's own report of what people actually search for, and which products they click. Free for brand owners.
Watch out: copying a rival's bullet points or photos is a fast way to get your listing suspended. Write your own, in the spelling of the target country (color, not colour, for the US).
9
Ship the boxes to Amazon's warehouse
A cargo agent moves the goods from the factory to Amazon's warehouse abroad. Someone on the far side must sign for customs and pay the import duty. Amazon will not be that someone.
India → sea or air → target country

In plain words

With Amazon's warehouse serviceFBA, you send your stock to Amazon once and they store it, pack each order, deliver it in two days and handle returns. You never touch a customer parcel. First you tell Amazon what you are sending; Amazon tells you which of its warehouses to send it to and gives you labels for every box.

Getting it there is a job for a cargo travel agentfreight forwarder. They collect from the factory, book space on a ship or plane, handle the paperwork at the Indian port, and deliver to Amazon's door. Amazon also offers its own door-to-door service for Indian sellersAmazon SEND. Sea is cheap and slow (six to eight weeks to the US); air is fast and roughly four times the price. Most sellers fly the first small batch and ship later ones by sea.

On the far side there is one role Amazon refuses to play: the person legally responsible for the shipment entering the countryImporter of Record. That person signs the customs forms and pays the import duty. You can be it yourself from India, with a US tax number and a customs bond, or you can pay a broker to be it for you.

Think of it like sending your luggage ahead to a hotel abroad. The courier (forwarder) carries it. But the hotel (Amazon) will not stand at customs to open your bags and pay the fees. Either you do, or you hire someone at that airport to do it in your name.

Why it matters

  • A shipment with no named importer sits at the port, racking up storage fees, then gets sent back or destroyed.
  • Duty is a real cost in your price. US duty rates on Indian goods changed several times in 2025 and 2026. Check the current rate for your product code before you set a price.
  • Amazon rejects boxes that break its rules: wrong labels, boxes over 50 pounds, loose items. The forwarder must know FBA rules, not just shipping.

Jargon decoded

FBA
Fulfilment by Amazon. Amazon stores, packs, ships and handles returns. You pay a monthly storage fee per cubic foot and a delivery fee per unit sold.
Freight forwarder
A company that arranges international cargo end to end: pickup, export customs, ship or plane, and delivery. Your travel agent for boxes.
Amazon SEND
Amazon's own door-to-door shipping service for Indian sellers exporting to Amazon warehouses abroad. Booked inside Seller Central.
Importer of Record
IOR. The legal name on the customs entry in the destination country, responsible for duties and rule-following. Never Amazon.
Customs broker
A licensed agent on the destination side who files the customs entry for you. Many will also act as your Importer of Record for a fee.
Customs bond
An insurance-style guarantee US customs requires from importers, promising duties will be paid. A yearly bond costs a few hundred dollars.
HTS code
Harmonized Tariff Schedule number. A 10-digit code that classifies your product and sets the duty rate. Your forwarder helps you pick it; picking wrong is costly.
DDP
Delivered Duty Paid. A shipping quote that includes duty and delivery to Amazon's door, so there are no surprises on arrival. Ask for it.
Shipment plan
The form in Seller Central where you declare what you are sending. Amazon replies with the destination warehouse and box labels.
Watch out: the old shortcut of posting small parcels duty-free into the US ended in August 2025. Everything now goes through customs with duty. Price accordingly.
10
Launch small, learn fast
Send a small first batch, pay for a little advertising, and earn your first honest reviews.
Online · Seller Central

In plain words

A new listing has no reviews and no sales history, so Amazon's search barely shows it. You break that deadlock in two ways. You pay Amazon to show your product when someone searches your keywordsSponsored Products, and you collect early reviews through Amazon's own programme where free units go to trusted reviewersAmazon Vine.

Keep the first batch small: a few hundred units. You will learn what breaks in transit, which photo people click, what questions they ask, and whether your price is right. Fix all of that before the big sea shipment.

Think of it like a soft opening for a restaurant. Invite a few dozen people, feed them well, fix the kitchen problems you find, and only then put up the big sign.

Why it matters

  • Reviews are the currency of Amazon. A product with fifteen honest four-star reviews outsells one with none, every time.
  • Ads on a listing with zero reviews burn money. Run a small budget first, then increase once reviews land.
  • Never buy fake reviews or offer refunds for reviews. Amazon closes accounts for this, permanently, with the money inside frozen.

Jargon decoded

PPC / Sponsored Products
Pay Per Click ads inside Amazon search results. You pay only when a shopper clicks. You choose the keywords and a daily budget.
ACOS
Advertising Cost of Sale. Ad spend divided by the sales those ads produced. If you spend 10 dollars to sell 40 dollars of goods, ACOS is 25 percent.
Amazon Vine
Amazon's early-review programme. You give up to 30 units free; vetted reviewers write honest reviews. Brand owners only. A small enrolment fee applies.
Request a Review
A button on each order in Seller Central that sends Amazon's standard review request. Allowed and free. Use it on every order.
Buy Box
The main "Add to Cart" spot on a product page. As the only seller of your own brand, you hold it by default.
Watch out: watch the return rate in your first month. Above about 5 percent means a real problem with sizing, quality or expectations set by the photos. Fix the cause, not the reviews.
Part 5

Get paid, and close the loop with India

The customer paid Amazon in dollars. Here is how that becomes rupees in your account, and why one bank receipt matters more than it looks.

11
Receive the money, keep the proof
Amazon pays every two weeks into your Indian account. Your bank hands you a receipt that proves the dollars arrived. Keep every one.
India · your bank and the trade portal

In plain words

Amazon collects from the customer, keeps its fees, and pays you the rest roughly every fourteen days. Because your bank account is in India, the dollars must be converted to rupees on the way. Amazon's own converter does this for a cut of a few percent; services like Payoneer or a bank's export account often do it cheaper. Either way, rupees land in the business account you opened in Step 4.

Every time foreign money arrives, your bank records it and gives you a receiptFIRA / FIRC. Then, on the foreign trade portal, you match that money to the shipment it paid for and generate an official closing certificatee-BRC. That certificate is the government's proof that the promise you signed in Step 4 was kept: the goods left, and the money came home.

Think of it like a library loan. The promise letter was you borrowing the right to skip tax. The bank receipt is you returning the book. No receipt, and the library assumes you never brought it back.

Why it matters

  • India's central bank expects export money to come home within nine months of shipping. The bank receipts prove it did. Missing proofs can block future shipments.
  • The closing certificate is also your ticket to export incentives: small cash-back schemes the government pays exporters, and any tax refunds you are owed.
  • Your accountant needs these to show the tax office why no tax was charged on your sales.

Jargon decoded

Disbursement
Amazon's word for a payout. Happens about every 14 days, minus fees, minus any money held for possible refunds.
Reserve
A slice of your payout Amazon holds back for a week or two in case of refunds. Normal for new sellers.
ACCS
Amazon Currency Converter for Sellers. Amazon's built-in dollar-to-rupee conversion. Convenient, not cheap.
FIRA / FIRC
Foreign Inward Remittance Advice or Certificate. The bank's receipt for each foreign payment that arrived, showing the amount, currency and sender. Ask your bank to issue it for every payout.
e-BRC
Electronic Bank Realisation Certificate. The official record, generated on the DGFT portal, that a specific shipment was paid for. Links a shipping bill to the money received.Since late 2023 exporters generate these themselves on the DGFT site using the bank's data.
EDPMS
Export Data Processing and Monitoring System. The central bank's tracker of open shipments. Every shipping bill stays "open" until matching money arrives and is reported.
RoDTEP
Remission of Duties and Taxes on Exported Products. A small government cash-back on exports, paid as credits, claimed against your shipping bills and closing certificates.
Watch out: if you use a payment service instead of a direct bank transfer, confirm they issue the FIRA in your business name. A receipt in the payment company's name does not close your shipment.

EIN
Employer Identification Number · US
A US tax ID for a business, free from the IRS. Foreign businesses apply by fax or phone with form SS-4. Also used when you are set up as importer.
IRS
Internal Revenue Service · US
The US tax office. Issues the EIN and receives the foreign-status form through Amazon.
W-8BEN / W-8BEN-E
Certificate of foreign status · US
The form that says "I live outside the US". BEN for individuals, BEN-E for companies. Stops Amazon withholding up to 30 percent of payouts. Filled inside Seller Central's tax interview.
FDA
Food and Drug Administration · US
Regulates food, supplements, cosmetics and medicines. Food factories register with it (with a US Agent), announce shipments in advance, and follow US label formats.
MoCRA
Modernization of Cosmetics Regulation Act · US
The 2022 law under which cosmetics makers register their facility and list each product with the FDA.
CPSC / CPC
Consumer Product Safety Commission / Children's Product Certificate · US
The toy regulator, and the one-page certificate you issue after an approved lab tests the toy against ASTM F963. Needed to open Amazon's toy category.
Importer of Record
IOR · destination country
The legal name on the customs entry, responsible for duty and rules. Amazon never plays this role. You do, or a broker does for a fee.
Customs bond
Import guarantee · US
A guarantee US customs requires from importers that duties will be paid. A continuous yearly bond costs a few hundred dollars.
HTS code
Harmonized Tariff Schedule · US
A 10-digit product classification that sets your duty rate. Your forwarder helps pick it. Rates on Indian goods changed often in 2025 and 2026, so check before pricing.
Country of origin marking
Import rule · US
A permanent "Made in India" on the product or its packaging. Required on every import.
Amazon Global Selling
Amazon's export programme for Indian sellers
The sign-up route that lets an Indian resident open seller accounts on Amazon's US, UK, European and other sites with Indian documents.
Brand Registry
Amazon's programme for brand owners
Free. Needs a trademark (filed or registered) and real photos of your branded product. Unlocks A+ Content, Vine, Brand Analytics and copycat takedowns.
FBA
Fulfilment by Amazon
You ship stock to Amazon's warehouse; Amazon stores, packs, delivers and handles returns. You pay storage per cubic foot and a fee per unit sold.
Freight forwarder
Cargo agent
Arranges the whole journey of your boxes: factory pickup, Indian customs, ship or plane, and delivery to Amazon. Ask for a DDP quote that includes duty.
Amazon SEND
Amazon's own shipping service for Indian exporters
Door-to-door from India to Amazon warehouses abroad, booked inside Seller Central. An alternative to a freight forwarder.
A+ Content
Enhanced product page section
The picture-rich part of the page below the bullet points, with banners and comparison charts. Brand owners only. Free.
ASIN
Amazon Standard Identification Number
The unique code Amazon gives every product page. Created when you list.
GTIN / UPC
Barcode number
The barcode on your packaging, one per product. Buy genuine ones from GS1 or get a brand-owner exemption. Reseller barcodes are often rejected.
PPC / Sponsored Products
Pay-per-click ads
Ads in Amazon search results. You pay per click, choose keywords and set a daily budget. ACOS is ad spend divided by the sales it made.
Amazon Vine
Early-review programme
Give up to 30 units free to vetted reviewers for honest reviews. Brand owners only, small fee. The legitimate way to get first reviews.
MOQ
Minimum Order Quantity
The smallest batch a factory will make. Negotiable, often for a slightly higher unit price.
Private label
Selling model
A factory's existing product sold under your brand. How most first-time Amazon sellers start.
